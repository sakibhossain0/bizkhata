## English

**App name** (30 max)
BizKhata: Stock, Khata & POS

**Short description** (80 max)
Stock, baki khata, sales and courier booking for your shop. Works offline.

**Full description**

BizKhata is the shop book for small businesses in Bangladesh. Keep stock, sell at the counter, write
baki (credit) in the khata, and send online orders to the courier, all from one app. Everything is
saved on your phone or computer, so it works without internet.

SELL FAST
• Counter sale (POS) with barcode scanning by camera
• Cash, bKash, Nagad, bank or card, or keep the rest as due
• Print receipts on 58 mm / 80 mm thermal printers or A4
• Send the receipt to the customer on WhatsApp as a picture or PDF
• Take back some or all items of a sale; stock and money adjust by themselves

STOCK
• Products with photo, unit (pcs, kg, litre, dozen…), barcode and low-stock alert
• Purchases from suppliers update stock and average cost
• Stock value at cost and at sale price; import or export your list with Excel (CSV)

KHATA (BAKI)
• Customer and supplier ledgers with running balance
• Due reminders by WhatsApp or SMS, with your bKash/Nagad number added for you
• Customer statement PDF

ONLINE ORDERS AND COURIER
• Book parcels with Steadfast, Pathao or RedX from the order screen
• Customer delivery record before you ship (Steadfast)
• Sync parcel status; delivered COD goes into your courier balance automatically
• Automatic SMS when an order is confirmed, sent or delivered (needs your own SMS gateway)

STAFF AND SECURITY
• Owner PIN lock, and a separate PIN for each staff member
• Cashiers can sell and collect dues but cannot see profit, cost prices or settings
• Every sale shows who made it; sales-by-staff report
• Locks itself after a few idle minutes if you want

REPORTS
• Day-end summary you can print before closing
• Sales, profit and loss, top products, stock, dues, expenses and cash book

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
• No account and no sign-up; nothing is uploaded to us
• Automatic daily backup, plus backup files you can share to Google Drive or another phone
• Several shops in one app, in Bangla or English

**What's new in 1.1.0**
Staff accounts with their own PIN and roles, partial returns, WhatsApp and PDF receipts, one-click
printing, a day-end summary, message templates with your bKash/Nagad number, automatic order SMS,
search everything with Ctrl+K, customer import from Excel, and many fixes.

## Data safety form (answers)

- **Does your app collect or share user data?** No. All shop data (products, customers, sales) is
  stored only on the device. The developer runs no server and receives nothing.
- Data the shop owner sends to a courier, an SMS gateway or WhatsApp goes there only when they
  press the button for it (user-initiated transfer to a service they chose), which Google exempts
  from "sharing".
- **Encrypted in transit:** yes (courier APIs use HTTPS). **Deletion:** delete the business in
  Settings, or uninstall the app.
